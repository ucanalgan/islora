# Islora 0.5.0-beta.2

- Fixed Settings opening invisibly when Islora was launched by a background startup process. An already hidden Settings window now becomes visible when opened again.
- Checked Caps Lock and Num Lock with a window-event probe on the development PC. Both showed Islora's pill without a second Windows or vendor popup. Other devices may show their own keyboard indicators.

This release does not change the volume and brightness flyout behavior introduced in beta.1.
